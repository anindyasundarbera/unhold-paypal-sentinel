Unhold Merchant System & Operating Guide

Autonomous Cash-Flow & Payment Protection Agent Team for PayPal Sellers.

💡 The Core Problem Solved

PayPal places temporary 21-day holds on seller transactions during sales volume surges, cross-border shipping, or when carrier tracking numbers are not linked to PayPal orders. For active merchants like Apex Home Goods, this locks up thousands of dollars ($5,235.24) of operational working capital.

Unhold is an autonomous multi-agent sentinel that pairs transaction event codes, reconciles carrier manifests, uploads tracking to PayPal Orders API, and slashes hold duration from 21 days down to ~24 hours post-delivery (accelerating $1,869.39 by 12.4 days).

1. The 3 Specific Slack & Zapier Automation Moments

Unhold connects directly to your operations team's Slack channel via outbound Zapier webhooks. Zapier does not run continuously in the background—it is precision-triggered at three specific, high-impact operational moments:

⚡ Moment 1: Batch Tracking Uploaded to PayPal (Cash Acceleration) Event: tracking.batch_uploaded

When it fires: Triggered the exact second the merchant clicks [Upload 38 Matched Trackers] in the Holds Ledger or AI Matcher tab.

What it does: Dispatches a rich Slack card to #unhold-alerts announcing that 38 orders were updated in PayPal, accelerating $1,869.39 USD by 12.4 days and boosting the merchant's Account Health Score from 53 to 73 (Fair).

Demo Tip: Point out your Slack notification arriving live right after clicking upload!

🚨 Moment 2: Phishing Scam or Ghost Payment Intercepted Event: payment.fraud_detected

When it fires: Triggered the exact second Payment Guard audits an inbound payment receipt email and discovers that the transaction ID is a 404 nonexistent phantom capture (e.g. CAP-FAKE-PHISHING-001) or comes from a spoofed domain (paypa1-security-alert.com).

What it does: Immediately sends an emergency security alert to Slack: "HALT SHIPMENT: Phantom payment notice detected for $820.00. Payment does not exist in PayPal."

Demo Tip: In the Payment Guard tab, load the phishing sample and click verify—Slack will instantly alert your warehouse team.

🤝 Moment 3: Autonomous A2A Dispute Settled Event: dispute.settled

When it fires: Triggered when Unhold's Buyer Assurance agent successfully presents the RFC 9901 SD-JWT cryptographic delivery proof to the buyer's AI agent, issues a courtesy store coupon, and mutually withdraws the dispute.

What it does: Dispatches a Slack notification confirming that dispute DISP-SEED-001 was closed amicably without human intervention, preventing chargeback fees and releasing withheld merchant funds.

2. System Architecture & Autonomous Agent Roles

  • Ledger Scribe: Reconstructs PayPal's transaction history by pairing T2107 (Payment Hold) with T2108 (Hold Release) event codes into a single verified ledger of 106 records.
  • Cash-Flow Strategist: Runs a 2,000-rollout Monte Carlo simulation forecaster and builds the dynamic Hold Waterfall schedule.
  • AI Tracking Matcher: 3-tier matcher reconciling disparate carrier manifests (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL) using exact IDs, deterministic name/ZIP inversion, and phonetic AI nickname equivalence (e.g., Adaeze $\to$ Ada).
  • Payment Guard: Audits inbound payment emails against live PayPal capture endpoints and enforces PayPal's $750 Signature Confirmation rule for Seller Protection.
  • Dispute Negotiator: Enforces prompt injection defense barriers against untrusted buyer inputs and negotiates 4-turn A2A settlements using privacy-preserving SD-JWT tokens.
  • Audit Officer: Maintains an immutable, SHA-256 sequential cryptographic hash chain verifying every single state transition from genesis.
